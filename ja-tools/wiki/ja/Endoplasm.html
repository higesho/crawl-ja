A glob of grey sludge that has somehow developed a rudimentary awareness and limited motility. It periodically draws heat from its surroundings to sustain itself, freezing enemies unfortunate enough to be adjacent at the time.

“Sea horses floundering in the slimy mud,
Tossed up their heads, and dashed the ooze about them.”
-John Dryden. _All For Love_, I, i, 15-17. 1678.

基本情報

Endoplasms are puddles of creeping, animated filth. Barely more durable than rats or frilled lizards, they are most notable for being able to Freeze their foes.

小技

  • Endoplasms are fragile and practically defenseless; even the naturally cold-vulnerable Djinn can usually kill them before they can pose any sort of threat.
  • Freeze doesn't miss and ignores armour; don't engage endoplasms in melee if you have 5 or fewer HP remaining.
