Jump ability

Leaps through the air in the blink of an eye. While it is impossible to control
your destination, the movement is so abrupt and agile that no monster will be
able to land a blow on you until you can act again, and many may lose track of
your position entirely.

You must spend a short while recovering between each use of this ability.
%%%%
Watery Grave ability

Turns all of the water near you into a surging tide that attempts to drown every
enemy standing in or above it. Those who need air to breathe will also become
Flooded, preventing them from casting spells affected by Silence for several
turns and dealing minor asphyxiation damage.

After using this ability, you must gain experience points before you can recover
enough power to use it again.
%%%%
Bestial Takedown ability

Unleashes the full power of your predatory instincts to leap towards a terrified
enemy and perform a vicious melee attack against them. This attack receives a
damage multiplier based on your Shapeshifting skill. If it kills them, you will
always perform a Howl afterward.

Can only target an enemy who is afraid, and who has at least one occupiable tile
adjacent to them.
