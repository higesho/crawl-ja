#   A primer on the elemental magics of Air. The pages throw off brief sparks as
#   they are separated.
# old ja (ja16):
#   風を司る魔法を学ぶための入門書だ。この本のページをめくるたび、

A primer on the elemental magics of Air. The great minotaur hero Vuxhurn, given
a copy of this book while trapped atop the tower of a wizard with a particularly
perverse sense of humour, used its pages to construct a rudimentary glider and
soared to freedom.

lightning rod

# old en (ja16):
#   This rod allows its wielder to fire continuous lightning: the discharge is
#   sustained, and can be redirected to new targets by evoking the rod in
#   subsequent turns (failing to do so will release the trigger). The rod takes a
#   moment to warm up, making short bursts rather ineffective.
# old ja (ja16):
#   このロッドで電撃を連続して浴びせることができる。
#   具体的には、一度発動した直後のターンにロッドを再発動して別の方向を狙うことで、
#   広範囲に放電することができる。
#   (そうしなければ引き金を放して単発の電撃を撃ったのと同じになる)
#   このロッドは威力が上がるのに時間がかかるため、単発の使用は効果的ではない。

A rod that allows its wielder to fire unavoidable blasts of lightning.
Consecutive evocations cause the discharge to be sustained, which increases the
power level up to four times and allows the blast to be redirected to affect an
area. The strength of the blast increases with Evocations skill and, for
redirected blasts, decreases with the size of the affected area.
